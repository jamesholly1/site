What We Offer

Every Service, Flawlessly Executed

From a quick refresh to a full paint correction, every detail is handled with precision. No shortcuts. If you're not satisfied, we're not done.

✦

Interior Detail

Deep vacuum, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, odor elimination, and full surface wipe-down. Your interior restored to showroom condition.

◈

Exterior Detail

Hand wash, clay bar treatment, paint decontamination, wheel and tire shine, and a protective sealant to guard your finish.

❋

Full Detail

The complete package — interior and exterior combined. Ideal before a sale, after a long winter, or when your vehicle deserves the best.

◇

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. Restores true depth and gloss to your paint.

⬡

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting nano-ceramic protection that repels water, contaminants, and UV damage for years. The ultimate investment in your paint.

◉

On-Site Service

All work is done at our garage in York, ME. Drop off your vehicle and we'll have it looking flawless — just let us know when works for you.
