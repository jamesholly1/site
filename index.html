About Me

A York Native Who Takes Pride In His Work

Porsche Cayenne — finished at the garage in York, ME

I'm Charles, and I've lived in York my whole life. I started Advanced Auto Detailing because I genuinely love what I do — and I think it shows in every car that leaves my garage.

When you bring your vehicle to me, you're not handing it off to a stranger or a chain shop. You're working directly with me, one on one. I'll remember your car, I'll remember you, and I'll make sure you leave happy every time.

I work on everything from daily drivers to high-end sports cars, boats, motorcycles, and RVs. No job is too big or too small — every vehicle gets the same level of care and attention.

I've been voted Best Auto Detailing in the Greater York/Kittery area five years in a row — an honor I don't take lightly. That recognition comes from this community, and it pushes me to keep raising the bar.

What I Offer

Every Service, Flawlessly Executed

From a quick refresh to a full paint correction, I handle every detail with precision. No shortcuts. If you're not satisfied, I'm not done.

✦

Interior Detail

Deep vacuum, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, odor elimination, and full surface wipe-down. Your interior restored to showroom condition.

◈

Exterior Detail

Hand wash, clay bar treatment, paint decontamination, wheel and tire shine, and a protective sealant to guard your finish.

❋

Full Detail

The complete package — interior and exterior combined. Ideal before a sale, after a long winter, or when your vehicle deserves the best.

◇

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. Restores true depth and gloss to your paint.

⬡

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting nano-ceramic protection that repels water, contaminants, and UV damage for years. The ultimate investment in your paint.

◉

On-Site Service

All work is done at my garage in York, ME. Drop off your vehicle and I'll have it looking flawless — just let me know when works for you.
